1. Who is this app for?
    1- Boxers, MMA Fighters
    2- Combat Coaches
    3- Individuals who want to learn how to fight

2. How does the app work?
Main Functions
    1- provide clips of a boxer
    2- app analyzes motions
    3- app finds trends and weak spots
    4- app gives advice on how to exploit weaknesses
    5- app is already trained on combat data beforehand
Extra Functions
    1- app creates boxing meal plan for a weight class
    2- provides animations of motions to perform
    3- round by round scoring
